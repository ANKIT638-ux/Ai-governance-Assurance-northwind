# AI Inventory — Northwind Retail
 
**Purpose:** A firm cannot govern what it doesn't know it's running. This inventory is the
foundation of the whole assurance system. Every risk, control and piece of evidence downstream
traces back to a system listed here. In practice, the biggest early finding in most real AI
governance reviews is an *incomplete* inventory (shadow AI, unregistered vendor tools, a team
that "just started using" a model). This inventory is treated as a living document, owned
jointly by each system owner and the AI Governance function, and reviewed quarterly.

## Inventory maintenance controls
- New AI system deployments require registration in this inventory **before** production go-live (gate owned by AI Governance, enforced via change-management ticketing).
- Quarterly attestation from each system owner confirming the entry is still accurate.
- Any change to data classification, integrations, or autonomy level triggers a re-assessment of risk tier (see `02-ai-risk-register.md`).
